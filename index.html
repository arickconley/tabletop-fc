Match notes

PRE-MATCH
    THE FIELD GUIDE

    One turn. One decisive play.

    Play Blue against the computer, or share this screen with a second player. Blue attacks right; red attacks left. The higher score after 20 normal rolls per player wins. Equal scores are a draw.

    01 / Roll & run

    Roll a six-sided die and choose one pawn. Move orthogonally, one square per step. You can turn or retrace, but never cross a pawn. You may end a normal turn early in any legal position; unused steps are discarded.

    02 / Strike on contact

    Reaching the ball ends your run. Kick in any of eight directions: the first square is free, then use up to the remaining die steps. Each kick can change direction once by 45°.

    03 / Chain a pass

    A teammate redirects immediately with a new free step and a fresh bend. Unspent die steps carry over. Each pawn kicks only once per turn. Defenders and used teammates block shots.

    04 / Find the net

    Cross the opponent’s end line from either goal-mouth square into the net. No other boundary exit is legal. Goals add to the score. After a goal, reset the starting formation and give the conceding team the ball at its center-ring pawn for its next normal turn.

    The opening whistle

    Each team rolls; ties are rerolled. The winner’s center-ring pawn kicks exactly the roll difference, with no free steps and no goal. The losing side takes the first normal turn.

    Ending a turn early

    After a normal roll, use End turn to stop without moving, partway through a run, or after kicking the ball onto an empty square. Unused points are discarded, and the roll still counts toward your 20.

    The position must obey the keeper, defensive-box, and opponent-path rules. You must kick on contact and redirect a received pass before stopping, and complete any required keeper-area relocation. Opening and halftime kickoffs must still travel their exact distance.

    20 rolls each & halftime

    Each player gets 20 normal die rolls, ten per half. Finish the current turn before halftime or full time. Kickoff rolls do not count toward the allowance, and undoing a move does not grant another roll.

    After both players finish their tenth roll, restore the starting formation and keep the score. The player who did not take the opening kickoff takes the halftime kickoff, regardless of which team rolls higher. Both roll for the distance (the absolute difference; reroll ties). No free steps or scoring on kickoff. The other player takes the first normal turn of the second half.

    Finish both players’ twentieth rolls before deciding the result. The higher score wins; equal scores are a draw. These match rules replace the PDF’s first-goal-wins format.

    Keepers & fair play

    Only your keeper may finish in your own goal mouth. A runner that kicks there must move to an empty adjacent square, free of charge. At most one runner can finish in your own 2×2 box. An opposing pawn must always retain an orthogonal path to the ball. Invalid turn endings need to be undone.

    Controls & setting up

    Click a pawn, then highlighted squares, or use the direction buttons. Gold marks a possible next step; a route can still become blocked later. Use Undo, or Reset turn in the ⋯ menu, to try another route without rerolling. Commit with End turn. Drag the pitch to orbit and scroll to zoom; use 2D in the ⋯ menu for a view from above. Direction controls in that menu show the arrow pad and pawn buttons; they are on by default on phones and hidden by default on desktop.

    Choose Computer as the Red player in the ⋯ menu to play solo as Blue. Red rolls and plays automatically; you start kickoffs and continue after goals. Choose Human to play both sides. You can change this during a match without starting over. Undo and Reset turn apply to your own turns.

    Manual setup alternates pieces, keepers first. Place exactly one runner in your half of the center ring, 2–3 runners on your own half and 1–2 outside the ring on the far half. The ready-made lineup matches the rulebook diagram.
